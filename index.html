THE CHAUPAL TABLE

More than a meal. A place to settle in.

At Seaview, the setting is part of the experience: warm rattan details, open-air seating, greenery and an unmistakably coastal backdrop. The restaurant is positioned as a family-friendly buffet destination where a group can explore different flavours in one sitting.

Public sources describe a menu spanning Pakistani/Desi dishes, BBQ, Chinese, Continental options, soups, salads and desserts. Menu selection can change by service and date.
